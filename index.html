About this app

›
Provides official Transport Service access to citizens across India through a mobile-based citizen charter application. This national platform empowers citizens with instant access to authentic vehicle documents, driving licenses, and RTO vehicle registration records. Key Features & Services: • RC Information Search: Enter any vehicle number to instantly verify owner name, registration date, registering authority, fuel type, vehicle class, and fitness validity. • Virtual RC & Virtual DL: Carry valid digital copies of your RC and Driving Licence backed by secure government cryptographic QR codes valid for police verification across India. • Second-Hand Vehicle Verification: Verify complete past registration and road tax history before purchasing used cars, bikes, or commercial transports. • E-Challan Search: Monitor pending traffic fines and settle road violations securely online.
